## Gap assessment and prioritization

Scoring uses 1–5. Higher is better for value, portability, source authority,
determinism, actionability, and false-positive safety; implementation cost is
also scored higher when the cost is lower. Only A-class candidates were
implemented.

| Candidate gap | Value | Portability | Authority | Determinism | Actionability | FP safety | Cost | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| NetworkManager activation failure | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | Implemented |
| Network-device TX watchdog | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | Implemented |
| Critical UPower power-source state | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | Implemented |
| Service/process coredump or crash loop | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 | Deferred: overlap ownership with segfault and failed-unit findings is not safely established |
| Storage mount reliability | 4 | 5 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 3 | Deferred: current `findmnt --verify` output is synthetic/unreachable in this environment and read-only mount state is often intentional |
| User session/display reliability | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | Deferred: `niri` is available, but a portable cross-compositor failure contract is absent |

The current machine probes support the selected contracts: NetworkManager is
active and its current-boot journal has no activation-failure marker; the
kernel watchdog query has no matching line; UPower exposes power-source records
with fully charged/non-critical state. The current machine also exposes
coredump records, but that does not remove the overlap boundary, so coredump is
not silently promoted into a new Finding.

## Implemented source contracts

### NetworkManager activation failure

- Source: `journalctl -b -u NetworkManager --no-pager 2>/dev/null`.
- Authority: successful current-boot NetworkManager unit journal only.
- Positive: exact marker `Activation: failed for connection`.
- Zero: authoritative command success with no exact marker.
- Near misses rejected: successful/activated text, generic connection failure,
  DHCP text without the exact activation marker.
- RAW: `NETWORK-NM-ACTIVATION-FAIL-001`.
- OBS/Finding: `NETWORK-NM-ACTIVATION-FAIL-001`.
- Evidence: `EVIDENCE-NETWORK-NM-ACTIVATION-FAIL-001-001`.
- Aggregation: exact duplicate lines are collapsed; up to 20 lines are retained
  in the bounded payload while the full match count remains deterministic.
- Failure/permission/missing/truncated: no health or failure inference on
  command failure; truncated positive evidence remains incomplete and produces
  `Guessing` confidence plus a `TRUNCATED_OUTPUT` restriction.
- Exact read-only recommendation tested:
  `journalctl -b -u NetworkManager --no-pager | grep -iP 'Activation:\s+failed\s+for\s+connection\b'`.

### Network-device TX watchdog

- Source: `journalctl -b -k --no-pager 2>/dev/null`.
- Authority: successful current-boot kernel journal only.
- Positive: exact `NETDEV WATCHDOG: <interface>: transmit queue <n> timed out`
  or `Detected Tx Unit Hang` marker.
- Zero: authoritative command success with neither marker.
- Near misses rejected: link-up, successful queue completion, generic network
  errors, and activation text.
- RAW: `NETWORK-DEVICE-WATCHDOG-001`.
- OBS/Finding: `NETWORK-DEVICE-WATCHDOG-001`.
- Evidence: `EVIDENCE-NETWORK-DEVICE-WATCHDOG-001-001`.
- Aggregation: exact duplicate lines are collapsed; the two independent exact
  signatures can be aggregated in one bounded observation.
- Failure/permission/missing/truncated: no health or failure inference on
  source failure; truncated positive evidence is incomplete and has `Guessing`
  confidence plus a restriction.
- Exact read-only recommendation tested:
  `journalctl -b -k --no-pager | grep -iP 'NETDEV WATCHDOG:\s+\S+:\s+transmit queue\s+\d+\s+timed out|Detected Tx Unit Hang'`.

### UPower critical power source

- Source: optional `upower -d`.
- Authority: successful structured UPower dump containing a
  `power supply: yes` device block.
- Positive: exact `state: empty` or `warning-level: critical`.
- Zero: `fully-charged`, `warning-level: none`, and ordinary discharging are
  not alarms; no percentage threshold is invented.
- Malformed: a power-supply block without a state/warning field is
  non-authoritative and produces a restriction.
- RAW: `POWER-SOURCE-CRITICAL-001`.
- OBS/Finding: `POWER-SOURCE-CRITICAL-001`.
- Evidence: `EVIDENCE-POWER-SOURCE-CRITICAL-001-001`.
- Aggregation: critical states from multiple power-supply blocks are counted
  in one observation; daemon-only/no-device output is not a failure.
- Failure/permission/missing/truncated: source failure is explicit and does
  not become healthy; truncated positive output is incomplete and has
  `Guessing` confidence plus a restriction.
- Exact read-only recommendation tested: `upower -d`.

## Blockers and deviations

- No blocker for the v0.4 development verdict.
- `coredumpctl` is available and exposes current-boot records via the explicit
  `--since "$(uptime -s)"` query, but the retained SIGSEGV population overlaps
  existing segfault ownership and service/process correlation is not safely
  established. It remains deferred rather than producing duplicate findings.
- `findmnt --verify` was not promoted into a rule: this environment reports
  synthetic/unreachable boot sources and many intentional read-only mounts.
- Session/display coverage remains niri-specific and report-only.
- Existing baseline restrictions, including dmesg restriction, Btrfs privilege
  limits, user-systemd source unavailability, and bounded systemd output, are
  preserved explicitly.
- This review establishes readiness for a separate v0.4 release decision; it
  does not perform that release.
